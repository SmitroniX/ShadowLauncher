Android Installation & Permissions

Shadow Launcher is distributed as a standalone, signed APK without third-party app store dependencies. Follow these steps to install:

  1. Download the official ShadowLauncher.apk to your phone or tablet.
  2. Open your Android file manager or notification shade and tap the downloaded APK.
  3. If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source.
  4. Tap Install and launch Shadow Launcher from your home screen.
Note on Android 11+ Scoped Storage: Shadow Launcher stores isolated Minecraft files in /Android/data/com.shadow.launcher/files/.minecraft by default, requiring no sensitive root or full disk access.

RAM & Heap Allocation Formula

A common mistake is assigning all available device RAM to Java. Android requires substantial RAM for the OS, surface flinger, audio server, and GPU textures. If you assign too much RAM, Android's Low Memory Killer (LMK) will force-close the game.

Device Physical RAM Optimal Shadow Heap Recommended Profile
4 GB Device 1536 MB – 2048 MB Vanilla 1.21 or Fabric + Sodium (6 Chunks)
6 GB Device 2560 MB – 3072 MB Fabric + Sodium + Lithium (8 Chunks)
8 GB Device 4096 MB (Sweet Spot) Sodium + Iris Shaders (10 Chunks @ 120 FPS)
12 GB+ Device 4096 MB – 6144 MB Heavy Modpacks (Create, Distant Horizons, Shaders)

Aikar's Flags & Garbage Collection

Garbage collection pauses are the #1 cause of frame drops in Minecraft Java. Shadow Launcher provides tuned GC presets:

1. Aikar's High-Performance G1GC (Default)

Best for general survival, Hypixel PvP, and modded play. Ensures predictable heap collection without sudden stutter spikes.

-XX:+UseG1GC -XX:+ParallelRefProcEnabled -XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=200 -XX:+UnlockExperimentalVMOptions -XX:+DisableExplicitGC -XX:+AlwaysPreTouch -XX:G1NewSizePercent=30 -XX:G1MaxNewSizePercent=40 -XX:G1ReservePercent=20

2. Shenandoah Low-Pause GC

Performs concurrent compaction, resulting in pause times strictly under 10 milliseconds. Highly recommended for high-refresh 120Hz/144Hz displays.

-XX:+UseShenandoahGC -XX:ShenandoahGCHeuristics=compact -XX:ShenandoahAllocationThreshold=70 -XX:+AlwaysPreTouch

Renderer Pipeline Comparison

  • VulkanMod: Renders Minecraft using modern Vulkan 1.3 commands. Eliminates CPU driver bottlenecks and delivers the highest sustained FPS on Adreno 6xx/7xx and Mali-G series.
  • Holy GL4ES: Highly-optimized OpenGL ES 3.2 translation layer. Best compatibility for older Forge modpacks (1.12.2 and 1.16.5).
  • ANGLE: Google's WebGL / GLES engine. Outstanding stability on devices with non-standard GPU drivers.

ShadowTouch Virtual Controls & Gamepads

Shadow Launcher features a complete on-screen virtual controller with custom touch zones:

  • Sprint Lock: Double tap the center D-Pad button or tap once while walking forward.
  • Tactile Haptic Feedback: Shadow Launcher utilizes Android's internal linear vibration motor for haptic clicks on jump, attack, and block place.
  • Bluetooth Controllers: Pair an Xbox Wireless Controller, PlayStation DualSense, or connect USB-C controllers (Razer Kishi / Backbone One). Shadow Launcher automatically maps analog sticks and bumpers without third-party keymappers.

Installing Iris Shaders on Mobile

Thanks to the pre-bundled Iris Shaders pipeline, running shaders on your phone takes just seconds:

  1. Download any lightweight shaderpack .zip (such as Complementary Reimagined or MakeUp UltraFast).
  2. Place the .zip into /Android/data/com.shadow.launcher/files/.minecraft/shaderpacks/ or click Install Shader in the Mod Hub.
  3. In-game, navigate to Video Settings > Shader Packs and select the pack.
  4. For smooth 60-120 FPS, set shader profile to Medium or Low with Shadow Distance: 6 Chunks.

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Game closes immediately upon clicking launch?

Ensure you have allocated at least 1536MB of RAM. If you are on an older Mali GPU device and experiencing a black screen with VulkanMod, switch the graphics renderer in the Turbo Optimizer tab to Holy GL4ES 1.1.5.

How to backup worlds and screenshots?

Your worlds are located in .minecraft/saves/. You can copy the entire folder using any Android file explorer (like ZArchiver or MiXplorer) or export them via the launcher settings.
